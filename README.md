    <h2>2. Covered Perils & Parametric Triggers</h2>
    <table>
        <thead>
            <tr>
                <th>Insured Peril</th>
                <th>Parametric Trigger Condition</th>
                <th>Required Third-Party Evidence Standard</th>
            </tr>
        </thead>
        <tbody>
            <tr>
                <td class="bold-cell">Medical Emergency</td>
                <td>Total absence from an exam paper due to acute, debilitating physical illness or emergency hospitalization.</td>
                <td>Official clinical discharge certificate or emergency room slip from a public or registered hospital dated the exact day of the exam.</td>
            </tr>
            <tr>
                <td class="bold-cell">Transit Disruption</td>
                <td>Unscheduled delays preventing arrival at the examination venue during the two (2) hours immediately prior to the exam.</td>
                <td>Official Transport Department or MTR service logs confirming a delay exceeding forty-five (45) minutes along the student's route.</td>
            </tr>
            <tr>
                <td class="bold-cell">Road Traffic Accident</td>
                <td>Unavoidable delay or missed examination due to a physical road collision during the morning commute.</td>
                <td>An official Police Accident Investigation Report timed and dated within the designated commute window of the examination.</td>
            </tr>
            <tr>
                <td class="bold-cell">Family Bereavement</td>
                <td>Absence from an examination resulting from the sudden death of an immediate family member.</td>
                <td>A certified copy of the death certificate of the parent, sibling, or legal guardian issued within seventy-two (72) hours of the exam.</td>
            </tr>
        </tbody>
    </table>
